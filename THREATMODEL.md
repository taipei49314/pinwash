Status values: **Open** (residual, no closing fixture), **Closed** (fixture exists and is named). A Closed row without a fixture is invalid.

A local v0 engine exists (not a Release, not on PyPI). Every residual row below is Open. Closing a row is a spec bump plus a named fixture: a test id in the suite as unittest names it (`tests.<module>.<Class>.<test>`), in backticks in the Fixture column. `tests/test_residual_rows.py` checks that this table and SPEC §11 list the same IDs, that every Status is Open or Closed, and that every Closed row names fixtures that are test ids in the suite.

Their wording is SPEC §11's, and their section signs refer to SPEC; the cited issues hold the members and the executed reproductions. Spec 13 gave R06, R07, R12 and R13 the hook-side `unknown_coverage` rows SPEC §3.3, §5.1 and §7 define; a row is visibility only and closes nothing.
